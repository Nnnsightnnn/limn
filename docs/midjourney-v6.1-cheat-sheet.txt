### MidJourney v6.1 Cheat Sheet

#### **Prompt Structure:**
``` 
[Subject] in a [Medium] style, [Environment], [Lighting], [Color], [Mood], [Composition] --[Parameters]
```

#### **Key Prompt Elements:**

**1. Subject:**  
Define what the image is about.
- **Person:** A medieval knight, a cyberpunk android, a woman in 18th-century attire  
- **Animal:** Majestic eagle, surreal underwater fish, glowing fox  
- **Character:** Fantasy elf, sci-fi robot, Victorian vampire  
- **Location:** Gothic castle, a bustling city, serene mountain range  
- **Object:** Ancient artifact, futuristic gadget, vintage camera  

**2. Medium:**  
Specify the artistic or photographic style.  
- Photo  
- Painting  
- Illustration  
- Sculpture  
- Doodle  
- Tapestry  

**3. Environment:**  
Set the scene where the subject exists.  
- Indoors  
- Outdoors  
- On the moon  
- Underwater  
- In the city  

**4. Lighting:**  
Control the light in your image.  
- Soft  
- Ambient  
- Overcast  
- Neon  
- Studio lights  

**5. Color:**  
Adjust the palette.  
- Vibrant  
- Muted  
- Bright  
- Monochromatic  
- Colorful  
- Black and white  
- Pastel  

**6. Mood:**  
Convey emotions or energy.  
- Sedate  
- Calm  
- Raucous  
- Energetic  

**7. Composition:**  
Frame your image.  
- Portrait  
- Headshot  
- Closeup  
- Birds-eye view  

---

#### **Parameters for Fine-Tuning**

- `--ar Aspect Ratio:` Define width-to-height ratio (e.g., `--ar 16:9`, `--ar 1:1`)  
- `--v` Version (e.g., `--v 6.1` for version 6.1 features)  
- `--q Quality:` Image quality, higher values improve detail (e.g., `--q 2`)  
- `--style` Artistic intensity or stylization level (e.g., `--style 4`)  
- `--no` Negative prompts to exclude (e.g., `--no background`)  
- `--chaos` Randomness factor (0-100 for variety in results)  
- `--tile` Enables seamless tiling for patterns  
- `--seed` Consistent results with the same input  
- `--testp` Testing parameter for Pro mode  

---

#### **Example Prompts**

**Example 1:**  
*"Cyberpunk android in a neon-lit city, photo style, outdoors, vibrant colors, energetic mood, portrait composition --ar 16:9 --q 2 --chaos 50"*  

**Example 2:**  
*"Majestic eagle in an oil painting style, flying over mountains at sunrise, warm ambient lighting, muted tones, serene mood, birds-eye view --ar 3:2 --seed 12345"*  

**Example 3:**  
*"Underwater coral reef scene, illustration style, soft light, pastel colors, calm mood, wide composition --tile"*  

---

#### **Tips for Advanced Prompts**
- Combine elements for complex imagery (e.g., "A Victorian vampire in a sci-fi city, tapestry style").  
- Use negative prompts to remove unwanted features (e.g., `--no humans`).  
- Adjust chaos for creative exploration (`--chaos 80` for wild variations).  
- Use seed numbers for reproducibility in iterative designs.  

This cheat sheet provides a flexible structure to build your MidJourney prompts and achieve a wide range of artistic effects!
